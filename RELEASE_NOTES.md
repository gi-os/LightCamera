## Roll v2.25 — Simple has one way of taking a photograph

**Size is a Pro setting, and Simple neither reads nor writes it**

Both halves of that were bugs. *Reading* it made Simple's behaviour depend on a choice made in Pro — so what
the shutter did was whatever Pro had been left set to, which on a cold launch is why nothing worked. *Writing*
it, which v2.24 did on entering the mode, changed a Pro setting behind your back and rebound the camera in the
process.

Simple now has exactly one way of taking a photograph: the frame already on the panel. It is the only
immediate path this camera has, it is what the coarse filters have always used, and there is nothing to
capture because the picture is already on the screen.

Panel resolution — real for sending and for looking at, not for cropping or printing. A still costs 1.8
seconds on this hardware whatever is asked of it, and that is what Pro is for.
